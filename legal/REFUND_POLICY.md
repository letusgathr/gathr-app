# GATHR — Refund & Cancellation Policy
**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Scope:** All Ticket Purchases, Upgrades, Add-Ons, and Event Registrations on GATHR.

---

## 1. Overview
At **GATHR**, our objective is to provide a seamless, transparent ticketing experience while safeguarding both Attendees and Event Organizers against arbitrary disruptions, fraud, and financial risk. Because GATHR operates as a ticketing and gate access technology platform, **the underlying refund policy for any given event is established by the independent Event Organizer**, subject to the mandatory platform standards set forth below.

---

## 2. Organizer Refund Policies
Each Organizer selects one of four standard refund tiers, which is prominently displayed on the event checkout page prior to ticket purchase:

1. **Flexible (Up to 7 Days Before Event):** Attendees may request a full ticket face-value refund up to 7 calendar days before the event's scheduled start time.
2. **Moderate (Up to 30 Days Before Event):** Attendees may request a full ticket face-value refund up to 30 calendar days before the event's scheduled start time.
3. **Emergency Discretionary Only:** Refunds are issued solely at the Organizer's individual discretion upon written petition for verified medical or personal emergencies.
4. **No Refunds:** All sales are final. No refunds will be granted under normal circumstances once the ticket purchase is confirmed.

> **Note:** If an Organizer fails to specify a refund tier, GATHR’s default rule applies: *All ticket sales are non-refundable unless the event is canceled or substantially postponed.*

---

## 3. Canceled, Postponed, or Materially Rescheduled Events

### 3.1. Event Cancellation
If an event is canceled in its entirety by the Organizer, venue authority, or government order:
- **Automatic Refund of Ticket Face Value:** All verified ticket purchasers are entitled to a **100% refund of the ticket face value**.
- **Payout Freeze:** GATHR will immediately freeze any pending payouts to the Organizer for that event and initiate automated refunds back to attendees' original payment methods.
- **Timeline:** Refunds are credited to attendees within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on the issuing bank or mobile money operator.

### 3.2. Event Postponement / Rescheduling
If an event is postponed or rescheduled to a new date or substantially relocated to a venue more than 25 kilometers (15 miles) away from the original venue:
- The Organizer must provide ticket holders a minimum **fourteen (14) calendar day window** to either accept the new date/venue or request a full refund of the ticket face value.
- If an event is postponed with no confirmed rescheduled date announced within **sixty (60) days**, the event shall be deemed canceled and automatic full refunds will be disbursed to all ticket holders.

---

## 4. Platform Service & Payment Gateway Fees
- **Standard Circumstances:** Platform service fees and third-party payment processing charges (charged by card networks, Stripe, or Paystack) cover the immediate cryptographic hashing, dynamic QR generation, and server bandwidth required to issue and safeguard passes. As such, **platform and processing fees are non-refundable**, except where the cancellation was directly caused by a verified malfunction or failure of GATHR’s core systems.
- **Organizer Fault:** When an event is canceled due to Organizer misconduct, deceptive marketing, or unexcused failure to perform, the Organizer is legally liable for all associated payment processing costs and chargeback fees.

---

## 5. Non-Refundable Scenarios
Refunds will **NOT** be issued under the following circumstances:
1. **Denied Entry Due to Venue Code Violations:** If an attendee is refused entry by venue security due to intoxication, disorderly conduct, dress code violations, or carrying prohibited items.
2. **Failure to Display Live Dynamic QR Pass:** Static screenshots, printed screen captures, or replayed photographs will fail door validation. Inability to present the genuine live dynamic pass due to a personal dead battery or lost phone does not qualify for a refund (though door staff can re-verify identity using the Organizer lookup tool).
3. **No-Show:** If an attendee fails to arrive at the event for reasons within their personal control.
4. **Secondary Market & Scalper Tickets:** Tickets acquired through unauthorized third-party marketplaces (e.g., ticket resale forums, Craigslist, unofficial WhatsApp groups) are void and strictly non-refundable.

---

## 6. Ticket Transfers & Resale
To provide maximum flexibility for attendees who cannot attend:
- **Free Ticket Transfer:** Attendees may transfer their ticket to a friend or colleague free of charge through the GATHR Web App up to **two (2) hours prior to event gate opening**.
- **Transfer Security:** When a transfer is executed, the original ticket QR code is instantly invalidated and a brand new dynamic cryptographic pass is issued directly to the recipient's email address.

---

## 7. How to Request a Refund
1. Navigate to your ticket receipt email or open the GATHR Web App under **"My Passes"**;
2. Click **"Request Refund / Manage Order"**;
3. Select your reason for requesting a refund and submit supporting documentation (if required);
4. The Organizer will review and respond within **forty-eight (48) hours**.
5. If an Organizer fails to respond to an eligible refund request within five (5) business days, GATHR Support reserves the right to review and adjudicate the claim.

For refund disputes, contact: `refunds@gathr.cloud`.
